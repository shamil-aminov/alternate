Alternate turns an Android phone into a MIDI keypad and plugs it into osu! over
a USB cable. The whole screen is one button. Every tap fires one of two notes,
alternating, so a single finger produces the alternating key presses a stream
needs, at speeds no single key can reach.

osu!lazer reads MIDI natively, so there is no bridge to install, no driver and
no key-mapping utility in the middle. One cable, one setting in the game.

The app is built around one number: the time from glass to MIDI. The note goes
out before any animation state is touched, nothing allocates on the path from a
touch to the MIDI byte, touch dispatch is unbuffered on Android 13 and up, and
the fastest display mode is requested at startup.

Features:

* Tempo gauge scaled to the tempos that decide whether you pass a map
* Chain and tap counters
* Adjustable debounce and MIDI notes
* Do Not Disturb while playing, so a notification cannot end a run
* Lite graphics mode for phones that drop frames
* Tap diagnostics: last interval, dropped taps, held notes
* No network access, no analytics, no accounts
